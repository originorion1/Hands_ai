# Outcome-learning evaluation

The benchmark freezes labels separately and holds period 2 out, but it has no
independently adjudicated, time-stamped outcome ledger for procurement or
inventory. ORION's retained missing-value investigation proposes a review
candidate; it does not create a business outcome prediction or promote
knowledge. A correction removes its candidate, and the wrong-tenant and
missing-authorization paths fail closed.

The existing ORION prediction ledger and F&B sample assessment are separate
capabilities with stricter witnessed semantic inputs. This fixture cannot
qualify their outcome learning. No post-outcome adaptation was run; no future
labels were fed into ORION. Consequently the measured improvement across
periods is **not established**. A qualifying follow-up would freeze a signed
prediction before each outcome, preserve immutable evidence references and
reviewer disposition, link outcomes after the cutoff, compare with a nonlearning
baseline, and check invalidations without unauthorized knowledge promotion.
