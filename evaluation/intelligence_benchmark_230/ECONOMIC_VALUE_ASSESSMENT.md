# Economic value assessment

This fixture estimates $83.40 of supplier price exposure across its two
injected price cases. That amount is **potential exposure**, not a verified
avoidable cost, recoverable amount, or realized saving. All three latter
categories are $0 verified in this synthetic evaluation. Duplicate invoice,
stock mismatch, and margin flags have no valid recoverability estimate here.

At the stated assumption of six analyst minutes per flagged unit and $45 per
analyst hour, reviewing nine conventional candidates costs an estimated
$40.50 and reviewing ORION's one candidate costs $4.50. The smaller ORION
review queue misses seven designed positives, so the $36 difference is not
an economic gain. ORION operating cost, implementation cost, customer review
cost, action cost, and false-negative loss are unmeasured. Net incremental
value and ROI are **not established**. No customer savings are claimed.
