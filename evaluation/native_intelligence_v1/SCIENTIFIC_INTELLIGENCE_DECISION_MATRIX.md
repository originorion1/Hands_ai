# Scientific intelligence method decisions

Decisions are for ORION's current read-only, small-data, governed setting. A method enters the Kernel only after it beats a simpler policy on a frozen, equally authorized evaluation. Authorization remains a hard filter before any utility calculation.

| Direction and primary reference | Assumptions and failure modes | ORION fit, engineering/compute cost | Minimal falsifiable experiment and simple comparison | Decision |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Bayesian experimental design — [Chaloner & Verdinelli, 1995](https://projecteuclid.org/journals/statistical-science/volume-10/issue-3/Bayesian-Experimental-Design-A-Review/10.1214/ss/1177009939.full) | Needs a calibrated prior and likelihood for each possible observation; wrong models can rank noisy or biased requests first | Fits bounded read selection when verified calibration data exist; moderate modeling work, low compute for finite questions | Freeze training outcomes; estimate likelihoods without holdout labels; compare expected entropy reduction per authorized request to coverage and random policies on independent organizations | **EXPERIMENT** |
| Active inference — [Friston, 2010](https://www.nature.com/articles/nrn2787) | Requires a defensible generative model and preference distribution; free-energy scores can conceal arbitrary utility choices | Current objective, likelihood and action models are too thin; high modeling and audit cost | Test whether it improves a fixed read-only decision metric over explicit Bayesian utility with identical observations | **DEFER** |
| Learning-progress curiosity — [Schmidhuber, 1991](https://people.idsia.ch/~juergen/curioussingapore/curioussingapore.html) | Repeated comparable learning episodes needed; noisy surprise can masquerade as progress | Existing coverage counters are not a measured learning curve; medium cost | Pre-register improvement in held-out prediction loss over time, compare to novelty/coverage heuristic at same read budget | **DEFER** |
| Causal hypothesis testing — [Pearl, 1995](https://proceedings.mlr.press/r0/pearl95a.html) | Causal claims require graph and identification assumptions, not mere association; hidden confounders can invert effects | Evidence-linked alternatives fit ORION, but ERP observations rarely identify interventions; medium review cost | Require a reviewer-specified causal graph and falsifiable counterfactual/negative control; compare to descriptive abstention | **EXPERIMENT** for reasoning, not causal claims |
| Predictive world models — [Ha & Schmidhuber, 2018](https://arxiv.org/abs/1803.10122) | Needs stable temporal dynamics and representative training; learned simulations can amplify bias | ORION has graph and prospective ledger, but not enough verified time series; high data and compute cost | Beat a seasonal/rule baseline on prospective cohort forecasts with provenance and no future leakage | **DEFER** |
| Calibrated uncertainty/selective prediction — [Guo et al., 2017](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v70/guo17a.html), [Geifman & El-Yaniv, 2017](https://papers.nips.cc/paper_files/paper/2017/hash/4a8423d5e91fda00bb7e46540e2b0cf1-Abstract.html) | Calibration shifts across tenants; small samples give unstable curves; abstention changes coverage | Ledger supports prospective scoring but not calibrated study utility; low to medium cost | Pre-register reliability bins, Brier score and risk-coverage at fixed abstention rules on independent outcomes | **EXPERIMENT** |
| Continual learning — [Kirkpatrick et al., 2017](https://www.pnas.org/doi/10.1073/pnas.1611835114) | Sequential updates forget old tasks; replay may violate retention; gradient regularization assumes a compatible model | Versioned revisions exist but no learned policy is qualified; medium to high cost | Freeze old domains, update only after adjudicated outcomes, then measure new gain and old-domain regression against no-update control | **DEFER** production; **EXPERIMENT** offline |
| Meta-learning/transfer — [Finn et al., 2017](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v70/finn17a.html) | Requires many diverse tasks; meta-training can memorize schema terms or generator rules | Current 12/32 synthetic cases are too small; high compute/review cost | Train on disjoint organizations, freeze weights, test unfamiliar terminology and data-generating families against non-transfer heuristic | **DEFER** |
| Metacognitive control — [Russell & Wefald, 1991](https://doi.org/10.1016/0004-3702(91)90015-C) | Value-of-computation estimates can be wrong; a learned stop rule can skip rare high-value findings | Explicit budget and abstention gates already exist; low cost for deterministic upper bounds | Stop when no authorized question can beat a conservative benefit bound; compare wasted reads and missed findings to fixed-cycle stopping | **ADOPT** the bounded deterministic gate; experiment before learning it |

The first engineering experiment should be finite Bayesian question selection **only when** independently verified priors and likelihood estimates are available. With no discriminating evidence, it must fall back to a transparent structural/coverage rule or abstain. It must never infer likelihoods from evaluation labels. The PR #280 nine-positive benchmark has no discriminating seed signal, so it cannot validate such a model; PR #281's structural coverage result is the simpler baseline to beat.
