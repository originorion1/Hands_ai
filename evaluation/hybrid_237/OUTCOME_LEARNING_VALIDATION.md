# Outcome learning validation

Each active outcome's period must follow its evidence period, and plans are
hashed before outcomes are loaded. This tests only chronology and leakage
boundaries for an offline evaluator. No prediction ledger, customer reviewer
adjudication, subsequent business outcome, adaptation step or knowledge
promotion occurs in this slice. The three-way benchmark has no new held-out
learning improvement. `OUTCOME_LEARNING = NOT_PROVEN`.

A later qualification needs separately custodied, time-stamped predictions,
immutable evidence digests, independent review, outcome linkage, invalidation
and a frozen non-learning comparator before any improvement claim.
